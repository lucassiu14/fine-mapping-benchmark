\title{\bf Fine-mapping across sparse and sparse-plus-infinitesimal architectures\\[3pt]
\large Iteration 004 benchmark --- complete analysis, all six strata}
\author{fmbenchmark}\date{\today}
\begin{document}\maketitle

\begin{abstract}\noindent
This report analyses the complete Iteration 004 fine-mapping benchmark: 45 design
rows, 11{,}250 scenarios, 112{,}500 region-fits per method, 18 methods, in-sample
LD throughout. It spans two genetic architectures --- a purely sparse model and a
sparse-plus-infinitesimal model --- crossed with three annotation conditions,
giving six strata that are never pooled. Every quantity derived from posterior
inclusion probabilities is defined explicitly before use, including its
aggregation level, its tie handling and the noise correction applied to the
variance decomposition. The principal findings are: (i) the number of causal
variants $S$ dominates the variance budget in every stratum; (ii) the
\emph{presence} of annotations matters and their \emph{strength} barely does;
(iii) the Functional BEATRICE ablation is monotone and highly significant in all
four annotated strata and \emph{exactly zero} in both unannotated ones, over
51{,}250 paired replicates; and (iv) the annotation-aware methods that lead under
the sparse model degrade sharply under misspecification, whereas the
region-sharing variant does not --- \texttt{fb\_xregion} rises from third to
second of eighteen and overtakes the entire PolyFun family.
\end{abstract}

\section{Design}\label{sec:design}

\subsection{The two architectures}

Each simulated region contains exactly $S$ causal variants. The arms differ in
what else contributes to the phenotype.

\paragraph{Sparse.} Only the $S$ causal variants have effects. Effect sizes are
drawn $\beta\sim N(0,\sigma^2_\beta)$ and the residual variance is calibrated to
hit the target heritability exactly:
\[
\sigma^2_\varepsilon=\operatorname{Var}(X\beta)\,\frac{1-\phi}{\phi}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathrm{PVE}=\frac{\operatorname{Var}(X\beta)}{\operatorname{Var}(X\beta)+\sigma^2_\varepsilon}=\phi .
\]

\paragraph{Sparse plus infinitesimal.} A second genetic component is added,
drawn on the \textbf{non-causal variants only}
($\alpha_{nc}\sim N(0,1/m_{nc})$, the BEATRICE formulation). The three variance
components are then scaled so that
\[
\operatorname{Var}(g_{\text{sparse}})=p_{\text{causal}}\cdot\phi,\qquad
\operatorname{Var}(g_{\text{inf}})=(1-p_{\text{causal}})\cdot\phi,\qquad
\sigma^2_\varepsilon=1-\phi .
\]
So $\phi$ is total heritability in both arms, and $p_{\text{causal}}$ is
\textbf{the fraction of heritability carried by the sparse component}. At
$p_{\text{causal}}=0.2$, four-fifths of the genetic signal is infinitesimal.

\paragraph{This has a consequence that governs how the second arm must be read.}
Under sparse-plus-infinitesimal, \emph{every non-causal variant has a genuine
non-zero effect}. The ground truth used for every metric in this report is the
set of $S$ sparse causal variants only. A method that correctly detects
infinitesimal signal is therefore scored as making a false discovery.
\textbf{All accuracy and calibration numbers in the second arm measure recovery
of the sparse component against a background of real but unlabelled signal},
which is the correct question for fine-mapping but is not the same question as
the first arm asks. Cross-arm comparisons of absolute FDR are meaningless for
that reason; comparisons of \emph{degradation} between arms are the informative
quantity, and are the subject of \S\ref{sec:misspec}.

Within every row: $S\in\{1,2,3,5,10\}$, $\phi\in\{0.05,0.1,0.2,0.4,0.6\}$,
10 iterations, 10 regions of nominal sizes
$\{500,500,750,750,1000,1000,1500,1500,2000,2000\}$, $n=5000$ individuals.

\paragraph{Causal variants are drawn with annotation-proportional probability.}
$w_j=\exp\!\big(A_j^\top\log\gamma\big)$, normalised, sampled without
replacement. \texttt{polyfun\_oracle} reconstructs exactly this $\pi_j$ from the
stored truth, which is what makes it a genuine ceiling rather than merely a
strong method.

\paragraph{Nominal region sizes are approximate at the top of the range.}
Regions are drawn from real 1000 Genomes loci, and many do not contain 2{,}000
usable variants. Measured actual counts:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}\toprule
nominal & median actual & ratio & within 5\% of nominal\\\midrule
500  & 499  & 1.00 & 100\%\\
750  & 749  & 1.00 & 96\%\\
1000 & 999  & 1.00 & 97\%\\
1500 & 1483 & 0.99 & 62\%\\
2000 & \textbf{1590} & \textbf{0.80} & \textbf{26\%}\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}

The true swept range is therefore $499$--$1590$ ($3.2\times$), not
$500$--$2000$ ($4\times$). Since $\omega^2$ is range-dependent
(\S\ref{sec:sensev}), region size's already-small importance is measured over a
narrower span than its label suggests. The design remains valid: variation in
actual size \emph{within} a nominal class is differenced away by the
$\sigma^2_u$ estimator (\S\ref{sec:vhow}) and correctly subtracted. Only the
label is approximate.

\paragraph{The unit of analysis.} A \textbf{cell} is
$(\text{row},S,\phi,\text{nominal region size})$, aggregating
$10\ \text{iterations}\times2\ \text{regions of that size}=20$ fits. There are
$45\times125=5{,}625$ cells per method. Cell means feed every ANOVA;
individual fits feed the calibration counts; replicate-level rows feed the
paired contrasts.

\paragraph{Stratification is not optional.} Nothing is pooled across
architecture or annotation type. Beyond the obvious --- a method that cannot use
annotations is not comparable to one that can --- the unannotated arms serve as
negative controls, and the two architectures pose different questions
(\S\ref{sec:design}).

\section{Completeness}\label{sec:completeness}

Every one of the 18 analysed methods has all $5{,}625$ cells populated, with
per-method declared-failure rates of $0.0\%$ and silent all-NA rates of $0.0\%$
across both arms. Three absences are structural and are \emph{not} defects:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Funmap} requires annotations and cannot run on either unannotated
arm. Its row there is \emph{empty}, not \emph{poor}; no performance claim may be
inferred from it.
\item \textbf{FINEMAP-inf} returns an all-NA posterior without raising an error
at $\phi=0.6$, $S=1$ in the sparse arm --- 45 cells, nine rows $\times$ five
region sizes. Recorded via a dedicated \texttt{bad\_pip} flag so the loss is
countable rather than averaged away.
\item \textbf{CARMA} was dropped mid-iteration on measured cost --- $19{,}000$\,s
per scenario against $9{,}765$\,s for the other eighteen combined, $66\%$ of
total runtime --- for a differentiator (LD-discrepancy outlier detection) that
an all-in-sample design cannot exercise. It is excluded from every analysis here.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{A prior failure, and what fixed it.} An earlier execution of the
sparse-plus-infinitesimal arm recorded $38$--$50\%$ failed fits for seven
Python-backed methods. The cause was environmental, not statistical: the
toolchain lived on a filesystem subject to a rolling 30-day purge and decayed
mid-run, so activation kept succeeding while imports inside it stopped
resolving. Those results were discarded and the arm refitted after moving the
toolchain to permanent storage. The run reported here carries a per-task
preflight that imports every module the tool sources actually use --- derived by
AST from the sources rather than listed by hand --- and aborts the task if any
fails. All $1{,}800$ tasks reported \texttt{preflight ok}.

\section{Overall accuracy}\label{sec:overall}

\begin{figure}[htbp]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figs/f01_ap_ranking.pdf}
\caption{Mean AP with 95\% intervals over the 5{,}625 cells of each method,
ordered by the sparse+infinitesimal continuous stratum.}\label{fig:ap}\end{figure}

Reading down the two rows of Figure~\ref{fig:ap}, three things change between
architectures.

\textbf{Everything gets harder.} Mean AP falls from $0.677$ (sparse) to $0.527$
(sparse + infinitesimal). This is expected and is not solely a difficulty
effect: the infinitesimal background is real signal that the metric counts as
noise (\S\ref{sec:design}).

\textbf{The leaderboard reorders.} Under the sparse model the PolyFun family
leads among deployable methods. Under misspecification it collapses and
\texttt{fb\_xregion} becomes the strongest non-oracle method on continuous
annotations. This is quantified in \S\ref{sec:misspec}.

\textbf{FINEMAP-inf comes into its own.} It ranks first or second across all
three sparse+infinitesimal strata. That is the architecture it explicitly
models, and the benchmark rewards it accordingly --- a useful internal check
that the second arm is doing what it was designed to do.

$S$ is the binding constraint in both arms: pooled AP falls
$0.780\to0.367$ from $S=1$ to $S=10$, a larger swing than any other factor
produces. $p_{\text{causal}}$ is the second lever in the infinitesimal arm,
rising $0.370\to0.640$ from $p_{\text{causal}}=0.2$ to $0.8$ --- unsurprising,
since it directly controls how much of the signal is the kind the metric scores.

\paragraph{Heritability is not monotone, and that is a real result.} Pooled AP
rises $0.482\to0.596$ from $\phi=0.05$ to $\phi=0.4$ and then \emph{falls} to
$0.586$ at $\phi=0.6$. The dip is not an artefact of the FINEMAP-inf gap --- it
survives excluding that method ($0.5907\to0.5811$) --- and it is
method-specific. Eight methods degrade at the top of the range, led by
\texttt{sbayesrc} ($-0.088$), \texttt{sparsepro} ($-0.078$) and
\texttt{finimom} ($-0.055$); ten improve, led by \texttt{susie\_inf}
($+0.032$). Methods whose priors or variational approximations are tuned for
moderate signal do not necessarily benefit from more of it. A benchmark that
reported only the pooled mean would have shown a flat top and missed this
entirely.

\section{Robustness to misspecification}\label{sec:misspec}

\begin{figure}[htbp]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figs/f14_misspecification.pdf}
\caption{AP under the sparse model (blue) against sparse + infinitesimal
(orange), annotated strata. Bar length is the cost of misspecification; methods
are ordered by it.}\label{fig:misspec}\end{figure}

This is the central result of the second arm. Taking continuous annotations:

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lrrl}\toprule
method & sparse & sparse + inf & \\\midrule
\texttt{polyfun\_est}   & \textbf{0.8061} (2nd) & 0.5480 & FDR@.9 $0.005\to0.35$\\
\texttt{polyfun\_ldsc}  & 0.7914 & 0.5443 & FDR@.9 $0.005\to0.38$\\
\texttt{fb\_xregion}    & 0.7933 (3rd) & \textbf{0.6521} (2nd) & FDR@.9 $0.049\to0.068$\\
\texttt{susie\_inf}     & 0.7398 & 0.6120 & FDR@.9 $0.005\to0.037$\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}

\textbf{The PolyFun family's FDR at $\pip\ge0.9$ degrades roughly seventy-fold},
from about $0.005$ to $0.35$--$0.38$, and its ranking collapses.
\texttt{fb\_xregion} loses accuracy too --- everything does --- but its FDR
moves only from $0.049$ to $0.068$, and it \emph{gains} rank, finishing second
of eighteen behind only the oracle.

The mechanism is visible in the design. PolyFun-style methods place a per-SNP
prior derived from a model in which annotation-weighted variants are causal and
everything else is null. When four-fifths of the heritability sits in unlabelled
infinitesimal effects that assumption is badly wrong, and a confident prior
built on it produces confident errors. The BEATRICE family's concrete
variational posterior does not commit as hard, and the shared prior head in
\texttt{fb\_xregion} is fitted across ten regions, which appears to regularise
it further.

\paragraph{The oracle is itself miscalibrated in this arm.} PolyFun-oracle
retains the highest AP but its FDR@$0.9$ reaches $0.36$--$0.38$, because it
holds the true \emph{sparse} prior while the infinitesimal component it does not
model generates real signal at unlabelled variants. The ceiling is a ceiling for
ranking, not for calibration, and $\kappa$ (\S\ref{sec:senseg}) inherits that
caveat.

In the sparse arm the trade-off is the familiar one: the BEATRICE family buys
ranking accuracy with roughly $2\times$ mass inflation and an FDR@$0.9$ an order
of magnitude above PolyFun's, while \texttt{susie\_inf} and \texttt{finemap}
stay near mass ratio $1.1$--$1.2$.

Under the infinitesimal model \emph{every} method's mass ratio rises, to
$2.5$--$4.2$. Part of this is genuine overconfidence and part is definitional:
mass placed on infinitesimal variants is real posterior belief about real
effects, counted here as excess. The two cannot be separated with this ground
truth, and the reliability diagrams in Figure~\ref{fig:rel} should be read with
that in mind --- the apparent overconfidence of the whole field in the right
panel is partly an artefact of scoring against the sparse component alone.

\textbf{What survives that caveat is the comparison between methods within the
arm}, since all are scored against the same truth. There,
\texttt{beatrice} ($2.51$) and \texttt{fb\_pooled} ($3.11$--$3.27$) are the most
conservative of the family and the PolyFun/SuSiE group the least
($4.2$).

\section{Sense V: what moves the metric}\label{sec:sensev}

\input{sec_vhow.tex}

\subsection{The variance budget}

\begin{figure}[htbp]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figs/f10_variance_budget.pdf}
\caption{The closed variance budget for AP. Strata whose design contains a
single row have no identifiable $\sigma^2_u$ and are omitted.}\label{fig:budget}\end{figure}

The noise floor runs $17$--$19\%$ in the strata where it is estimable, with main
effects at $60$--$68\%$ and everything above two-way under $5\%$. That is the
licence for reading the marginal plots of \S\ref{sec:conditional} at face value.

\paragraph{Two strata have no budget at all.} \texttt{sparse/none} contains a
single design row, so there is only one group from which to estimate
$\sigma^2_u$ and the between-region-draw component is unidentifiable. Every
corrected share in this section therefore excludes it. Uncorrected shares would
be inflated, particularly for whole-plot terms, and are not reported.

\subsection{Different metrics have different drivers}

\begin{figure}[htbp]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figs/f11_sense_v_responses.pdf}
\caption{Noise-corrected $\omega^2$ for three responses. The factor ordering
changes with the response.}\label{fig:vresp}\end{figure}

Ranking accuracy and mass calibration are both governed by $S$; false discovery
is governed by $\phi$. A benchmark reporting AP alone would call $\phi$
second-order, when for the metric a practitioner acts on --- the error rate
among confident calls --- it is the leading factor.

\paragraph{Negative shares are reported unclamped.} Where
$\mathrm{df}_T\lambda_T$ exceeds $\mathrm{SS}_T$ the corrected share goes
negative, which says the estimated noise for that term is larger than the
variation observed. FDR at a fixed threshold is a ratio of small counts and is
far noisier per cell than AP, so its decomposition is unreliable at this
replication level. Clamping would hide exactly that diagnostic.

Region sharing is worth most where regions contain fewest causals and decays as

\subsection{$\kappa$ is reported but no conclusion rests on it}

After gating, $\kappa$'s standard deviations exceed unity against means well
below it. The denominator is small in many surviving cells and the ratio is
correspondingly unstable. Every conclusion in this section rests on the
controlled pairs, which are differences rather than ratios.

\paragraph{The $\kappa$ variance decomposition is unavailable.} The
replicate-level table does not carry the region index, so $\sigma^2_u$ cannot be
estimated for the derived $\kappa$ and $\Delta$ responses. The code refuses to
report an uncorrected decomposition, which would inflate whole-plot terms by
$m\sigma^2_u/2$ per degree of freedom.

\section{Validity checks}\label{sec:validity}

Mechanically verified on this run; all pass.

\begin{enumerate}
\item In-sample LD only --- zero rows carrying a reference-panel size.
\item 45 design rows present, matching the grid.
\item Balanced cells per analysed method --- exactly $5{,}625$ for all 18.
\item CARMA excluded by design and reported as such.
\item No method entirely absent outside the unannotated arms.
\item \texttt{polyfun\_est} $=$ \texttt{susie} on both unannotated arms, exactly.
\item \texttt{polyfun\_ldsc} $=$ \texttt{susie} on both unannotated arms, exactly.
\end{enumerate}

Additional checks performed for this report, beyond the pipeline's own:

\begin{enumerate}\setcounter{enumi}{7}
\item \textbf{Truth consistency}: $\texttt{n\_causal}=S$ for all $2{,}031{,}230$
fit rows; zero mismatches.
\item \textbf{Grid completeness}: exactly $11{,}250$ distinct
$(\text{row},S,\phi,\text{iter})$ combinations; \texttt{region\_idx} takes only
the values 1 and 2.
\item \textbf{Metric internal consistency}: $\mathrm{tp}_t\le n_t$ and
$\mathrm{tp}_t\le S$ at every threshold; $n_t$ monotone decreasing in $t$;
$\mathrm{AP}\in[0,1]$; $|CS_{95}|\le p$; band counts summing to $p$; causal band
counts summing to $S$. Zero violations in every case.
\item \textbf{Negative controls, exactly}: all four BEATRICE variants identical
to machine precision on both unannotated arms (max $|{\rm diff}|=0$ over 625
cells), and \texttt{polyfun\_\{est,ldsc,oracle\}} identical to \texttt{susie}
there.
\item \textbf{Monotonicity where it should hold}: AP strictly decreasing in $S$;
strictly increasing in $p_{\text{causal}}$; sparse arm easier than
sparse+infinitesimal. AP is \emph{not} monotone in $\phi$, which
\S\ref{sec:conditional} shows to be a genuine method-specific effect rather than
a fault.
\item \textbf{Simulation code}: PVE calibration verified to be exact by
construction in both architectures; the oracle prior verified to use the
identical formula to the causal-selection weights.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limits}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{In-sample LD only.} Iteration 003 established that in-sample and
reference-panel LD can invert method rankings. Nothing here transfers to the
reference-panel setting.
\item \textbf{The infinitesimal arm scores the sparse component only.} Non-causal
variants carry real effects that count as false discoveries. Absolute FDR is not
comparable across arms; degradation between arms is.
\item \textbf{Nominal region sizes are approximate above 1000}, with the 2000
class median 1590.
\item \textbf{Range dependence.} All importance statements are relative to the
ranges swept.
\item \textbf{Per-cell decisions are underpowered} (\S\ref{sec:sensed}).
\item \textbf{Two strata lack a noise floor} and are excluded from Sense V.
\item \textbf{$\kappa$ decomposition unavailable}; the replicate table lacks the
region index.
\item \textbf{One tuning configuration.} BEATRICE-family hyperparameters come
from a single Optuna study and are held fixed; competitors run at documented
defaults. A separate analysis found the family's sensitivity to those
